# AMPAM — Moroccan Law Archive

A documentary archive of the legal and historical positions published by AMPAM
(Allodium Moroccan Praedium Ante Michigan), founding member state of the Moroccan State Union.

**Site: https://ampamgov.org** · **Platform overview:** [`ampam-platform`](../../ampam-platform)

---

## What this is

A published record of the treaty instruments, historical sources, and legal positions the project
is built on, together with the project's own commentary on them.

This is an archive. It states the project's positions as its own. It does not claim recognition by
any government or court, does not process filings, and does not provide legal advice or
representation to anyone.

## Editorial standard

Material in this archive is labeled as either **documented fact** or **the project's
interpretation**. Contested historical and legal material is not presented as settled. Where a
source is quoted, it is quoted as written and attributed; where the project draws a conclusion from
it, that conclusion is identified as the project's own.

Present national designation used throughout is **Moor** / **Moroccan** (Al Maurikanos). Historical
designations appearing in reproduced source documents are left verbatim as part of the record.

## Scope

**In scope:** treaty texts and citations, historical sources, published position papers,
constitutional and governance documents, and the project's commentary.

**Not in scope:** operational tools, form generators, instrument builders, or anything that
produces a filing. This repository is a reading archive.
